Zakaria Rafi.

Cloud systems · Machine learning · Networks

I’m an undergraduate student in the Faculty of Computer Science at Universitas Brawijaya. I’m interested in systems research, especially at the intersection of cloud computing, machine learning, and computer networks.

I want to understand how distributed infrastructure, learning workloads, and network design shape the performance and reliability of real-world systems. I’m particularly curious about efficient ML serving, resource management in the cloud, and the trade-offs between latency, throughput, and cost.

I’m part of the System and AI Research Training Class taught by Professor Haryadi Gunawi from the University of Chicago, through the Garuda ACE Community.

As a student, I’m building my research foundations by reading papers, experimenting with code, and sharing what I learn. I’d be glad to connect with people working on cloud systems, ML infrastructure, or networking.
