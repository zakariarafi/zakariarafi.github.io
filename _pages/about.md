I’m an undergraduate student in the [Faculty of Computer Science](https://filkom.ub.ac.id/) at [Universitas Brawijaya](https://www.ub.ac.id/id/). I’m interested in **systems research**, especially at the intersection of cloud computing, machine learning, and computer networks.

I want to understand how distributed infrastructure, learning workloads, and network design shape the performance and reliability of real-world systems. I’m particularly curious about efficient ML serving, resource management in the cloud, and the trade-offs between latency, throughput, and cost.

I’m part of the System and AI Research Training Class taught by [Professor Haryadi Gunawi](https://people.cs.uchicago.edu/~haryadi/) from the University of Chicago, through the [Garuda ACE Community](https://sites.google.com/site/garudailmukomputer/about).

As a student, I’m building my research foundations by reading papers, experimenting with code, and sharing what I learn. I’d be glad to connect with people working on cloud systems, ML infrastructure, or networking.
